## Before you start

The board boots from eMMC by default. Press the USR button at power-on to boot from the microSD card.

## Installation

1. Hold the USR button.
2. Connect USB-C power.
3. Release the USR button when the power LED comes on.

## Notes

- The SD boot selection stays until power off. Repeat steps 2 to 4 at each power-on.

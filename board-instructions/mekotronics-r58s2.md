## Before you start

Write Armbian to the eMMC with `rkdeveloptool` in MaskROM mode.

## Installation

1. Put the board in MaskROM mode.
2. Connect the board to your computer with USB.
3. Write the image with `rkdeveloptool`. See [Writing the image](https://docs.armbian.com/getting-started/writing-the-image/#rockchip).

## Before you start

- The C.H.I.P. has no microSD slot. This image does not boot from the onboard NAND.
- The image needs a microSD slot on an MMC2 breakout board. The breakout board connects to the NAND signals of the SoC.

## Installation

1. Install the MMC2 breakout board.
2. Insert the card into the breakout board and power on.
